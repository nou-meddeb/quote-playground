# Quote Playground

A clickable prototype of a quote and pricing editor, reconstructed from an in-house pricing platform I designed as a Business Consultant (Product Owner scope) at a B2B technology company. Company, products and prices are anonymized and illustrative.

**Live prototype:** https://nou-meddeb.github.io/quote-playground/
**45-second walkthrough:** [demo.mp4](demo.mp4)

## The problem it solved
Creating one quote took 7+ steps and up to six people: a slow, over-customized CRM, a separate product platform, sequential approvals, legal reviews through separate tickets, and manual quote fixes that broke invoicing later.

## What the prototype shows
- **One shared editor:** all line items on one screen, with live price preview before anything is saved.
- **Discount guardrails:** up to 15% approves automatically, 15 to 30% needs the approver, above 30% is blocked.
- **Approver in the session:** the sales director joins the quote and approves there, instead of a back-and-forth loop.
- **Customer PDF rules:** positive % discounts are shown; surcharges and hand-edited totals show only the net amount.
- **Item text:** text added under a line item carries through to the PDF.
- **Save before PDF:** the PDF is only available for the saved version, so it always matches the system data.
- **Change history:** every change logs who did what.

## Outcome of the real platform
Roughly 3x faster time-to-market for new offers, adopted by 30+ users across four to five teams.
