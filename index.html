Try this (2 minutes)

  1. Set the licence discount to 20. The quote now needs approval.
  2. Click Invite approver. The sales director joins and approves in the same session.
  3. Type a line total by hand, or a negative discount. Check the PDF: neither is shown to the customer.
  4. Add text under an item, then Save and Download PDF. Edit anything and the download disappears until you save again.

    Portfolio prototype by Nouha Meddeb, reconstructed from a pricing platform I designed. Products, prices and rules are illustrative. Rules shown: discounts up to 15% approve automatically; 15 to 30% need the approver; above 30% are blocked; negative discounts (surcharges) are allowed; a one-time fee needs a licence on the quote. The customer PDF shows a discount only when it is positive and entered as a percentage; surcharges and hand-edited totals show as the net amount only.
